### Two honest warnings about the digital side

1. **Editorial orientation.** Voicepk.net is rights-focused and, judging by its coverage, critical of the amendment. If the study finds that "digital media" was more critical than *Dawn*, an examiner may ask whether that came from **the medium** or from **this outlet's editorial stance**. The thesis must not claim that the medium caused the differences. RQ3 and RQ4 already use cautious wording, and Chapter 3 will list this as a limitation.
2. **Possible shortage of hard news.** Voicepk.net may not have four hard-news reports in the window. If it does not, there are two fall-backs: (a) replace it with The Friday Times, or (b) take 5 reports from The Current and 3 from Voicepk.net.

### How to tell if a *Dawn* text is from the print edition

On Dawn.com, print-edition stories (a) are labelled **"Newspaper"** in the section tag, and (b) end with the line **"Published in Dawn, [date]"**. Both signs were seen in search results (e.g. Dawn.com/news/1866542, "Published in Dawn, October 21st, 2024"). Stories labelled "Pakistan" without that line are **web-only** and are **excluded**, because they would bring back the overlap problem.

## 4. Data-collection procedure (step by step)

1. Open each candidate URL and confirm: date inside the window, hard-news genre, at least 300 words, and (for *Dawn*) the "Published in Dawn" line.
2. Copy the full text into a Word file named by its ID (e.g. `D01_Dawn_2024-10-21.docx`). Keep the headline, byline, date and every paragraph exactly as published.
3. Save the page as a PDF and submit the URL to the Internet Archive (web.archive.org → "Save Page Now"). Record the archive link.
4. Enter the details in the **corpus log** (Section 5).
5. Number every paragraph (P1, P2, …) so that quotations in Chapter 4 can be located exactly (APA allows "para." locators for unpaginated sources).
6. When 16 texts are selected, send me the texts (or let me fetch them if access is enabled). I will analyse only texts that have actually been collected.
